# OpsCopilot Demo Institute

Use the email address assigned to your institute account and select its assigned role.

- Existing account: sign in with email and password, or use Google with the same email.
- New student account: a counselor or owner must first add the student profile with the student's email. Create the account with that email, verify the emailed OTP, and the student profile will be linked automatically.
- Other new roles require an active registration invitation from the institute.

After authentication, the app checks the database role and opens only that role's dashboard.
